# Final project closure

Closed by the owner on **27 October 2026**. Both product ideas are retained as research projects. Development and startup exploration are discontinued. Historical plans are context only.

## What is preserved

- SoftSpace V27: six full STEP assemblies, 42 solids each. All 189 recorded nominal, adjustment and folding samples passed the chosen intersection checks; wearing stops also blocked the recorded queries. These are finite rigid geometry checks.
- Flex V27: 18 mm and 32 mm soft-zone CAD references, matching the sourced 12.7 mm foam specimen thickness. Both three-solid references passed independent validity and nominal nonoverlap checks.
- Flex alignment screen: 840 declared scenarios; 799 comparable pairs and 41 unpaired cases outside the supported comparison domain. The 18 mm zone lowered mean arm pressure in 279/400 comparable cases and increased it in 121. The 32 mm zone lowered it in 399/399 comparable cases. These are model comparisons, not people or success probabilities.
- Eight named Confor loading curves, earlier material screens, all older CAD, numerical evidence, failed solver attempts and reports remain preserved.

## What did not become a validated result

The relieved joint has no newly accepted equilibrium field. A checkpoint residual of 7.5353e-6 missed the strict 1e-7 gate; its stress output is diagnostic only. Later inexact, stronger-block and coarse-correction attempts did not resolve that gate. A diagnostic cleanup NameError occurred after saving the diagnostic field. A full-to-local correspondence-check launch failed to import its module, so no integration-correspondence result is claimed.

SoftSpace did not implement an electronic ANC system. The later ambition of premium ANC below INR 5000 was not achieved. There are no physical prototype measurements, wearer results, calibrated acoustic ratings, proven pain relief, verified Sony mounting, production cost or manufacturing release. A continuous nominal cover is not evidence of zero leakage. Source-based pressure models do not validate a finished pad.

## Research context at closure

The 2016 [earmuff versus ANC study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6091992/) does not demonstrate a majority preference for ANC. Newer [2025 tunable ANC research](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-89609-6) and a related [2026 parent-report follow-up](https://research.polyu.edu.hk/en/publications/auditory-perception-based-tuneable-noise-control-approach-for-imp/) support further investigation of personalised sound reduction, not SoftSpace performance. Related samples must not be assumed independent.

For glasses, the [2019 earmuff fixture study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6539177/) supports the effect of spectacle arms on acoustic seals. The [3M technical bulletin](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/2531501O/tb-attenuation-characteristics-peltor-earmuffs-eyewear-en.pdf) provides manufacturer test evidence. Earmuff losses cannot be transferred as exact music-headphone results.

Existing solutions include [Turtle Beach adjustable ProSpecs relief](https://support.turtlebeach.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/115001384573-Elite-Pro-PC-Edition-How-to-Remove-Ear-Cushions-Adjust-ProSpecs-Glasses-Relief), [Noisefighters Sightlines](https://noisefighters.com/collections/hearing/products/sightlines1) and [OcuClips](https://ocuclips.com/). ProSpecs is not merely ordinary memory foam. Neither novelty, absence of Indian competition nor freedom to operate was established.

## Archive meaning

**The project packaging is finished. Missing tests have not been renamed as passes.** This archive preserves a student engineering investigation and its limitations. Historical purchase plans, to-do lists and release gates do not authorise automatic further work. The owner may explicitly reopen the project later.
