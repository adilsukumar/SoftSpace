# SoftSpace

**Closed research project — 27 October 2026.**

An adjustable over-ear mechanical and passive sound-reduction concept, originally focused on sound-sensitive autistic users. The later premium ANC ambition was not implemented: this project contains no functioning ANC electronics or validated controller.

All 189 latest nominal, adjustment and folding samples passed their chosen geometric checks. Wearing-stop queries also passed. Joint strength remains unresolved. Geometry passes do not prove comfort, sensory acceptance, attenuation, durability or manufactured fit.

No further development is planned. [Earlier route README](../docs/history/2026-10-27/SoftSpace_README.md) and older test records are preserved as history. [Back to both projects](../README.md).
