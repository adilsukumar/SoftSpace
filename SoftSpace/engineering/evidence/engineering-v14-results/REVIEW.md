# V14 engineering review

This is a local STEP candidate, not a manufacturing release. Read test_register.json and release_review.json before using the files.

The failed V13 radial assembly is retained as a rejected revision. V14 changes to rear cap insertion with a separate pull knob, but the actual retained knob connection, threads, torque, fastening and complete joint contact are still unspecified or unvalidated. An assembly sample is not a real spring handling trial.

Child medium and large become slightly larger when folded. Child portability is not solved. V12 band and fork results remain V12; V14 has only the explicitly recorded ideal fork fixture. Physical comfort, sensory acceptance, heat, acoustic isolation, leakage, falls, folding cycles and ageing remain untested. Sony mount measurements were deferred by the user.

Earlier V14 export and FE attempts failed allocation. Sequential fresh process reruns replace them only where the final records pass. Coincident component Boolean equivalence remains unverified, so no inherited complete FE qualification is claimed. Native Fusion archives remain V10.

The paired Word reports include dated selected competitor evidence, not every market model or current checkout prices. No zero leakage, pain relief, unbreakable, lowest cost or superiority claim has been established.
