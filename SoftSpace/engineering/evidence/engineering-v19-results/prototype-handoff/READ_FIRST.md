# V19 prototype handoff

This packet closes a bounded digital prototype iteration. It is not a production release or proof that autistic users will accept it. Native editable Fusion archives remain V10. Current V19 files are local STEP assemblies.

## Assembly proposal
1. Select traceable structural, friction washer, spring, foam and cover samples. The earlier 70 material families and ten numerical grade references are screens, not tests of every material. No final foam or cover has been selected.
2. Use the STEP parts table and key dimensions for a prototype supplier review. General envelopes are not complete machining drawings. Define critical datums at the pivot axis, fork contact faces and slider guide before manufacture.
3. Proposed custom pin has a 4 mm plain bearing shoulder, 37 mm child or 57 mm adult, followed by an 8 mm M4 x 0.7 threaded end. Underhead length is 45 or 65 mm, head envelope 7 mm by 4 mm. Thread geometry is cosmetic. Head drive, runout, edge breaks, ISO fit classes, strength and supplier tolerances still need an agreed drawing. Do not manufacture from the failed exact-helix trial.
4. Retain the two friction washers, backups and two nested same-direction disc springs shown in the CAD. Two discs provide a published reference force of 236 N at the same reference shortening; this is not a measured assembly preload.
5. Adjust on a bench using a direct torque measurement, provisionally 160 Nmm with a chosen 128 to 192 Nmm comparison window. Measure both directions and running torque. This target is an engineering trial, not a sensory comfort or safety limit.
6. Hold the primary 7 mm across-flats nut and countertighten a second 2.2 mm high jam nut with two tools. Recheck torque after locking. CAD checks only the nut envelopes, not wrench access, locking effectiveness or tightening-induced load. Tip protrusion is nominally 1.9375 mm, before tolerances and spring setting change.
7. Slider guides, end stops, pad seams, cover bond, cup attachment and crown padding require sample assembly checks. Proposed rear flange bonding keeps a seam away from the skin contact face, but adhesive, seam strength, skin compatibility and sound sealing are unqualified. Do not cut an open glasses slot through the continuous cover.

## Physical work in order
First collect the material curves and Sony dimensions in the blank forms. Make one bench hinge and pad sample, measure and calibrate the models, then make a complete adult fixture prototype. Check fit, locking and sharp edges before wearer trials. Use several glasses arms, repeated seating, original versus uniform replacement versus soft-zone pads, matched inside-cup playback level and ANC on/off for the Sony. Children and autistic users need voluntary participation, easy removal and individual feedback. Computer pressure maps cannot provide that feedback.

## Digital work that remains input dependent
Full worn multi-material contact, calibrated nonlinear foam and cover response, real bearing and thread strength, long-term creep and fatigue, broadband sound isolation, outgoing music leakage, heat and moisture prediction, exact Sony mounting fit, manufacturing tolerances and quotations cannot be validated using the blank forms. No computer pass is recorded for these items. Earlier proxy models and failures remain in the reports.

## Hinge and material findings
The proposed holding window doubles V18. Its worst assumed equivalent-static threshold is about 2.30 g rather than 1.15 g. This is not an impact or drop result. A larger holding torque may make folding harder. PORON reference points give large forces at uniform 25 percent compression over the annulus; a product called very soft is not automatically a suitable full cushion. No novel material winner, budget advantage or superiority over the market is established.
